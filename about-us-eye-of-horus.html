Our Knowledge of Casino Games and Slots

Slots are a major part of online casino play, but they are often described in vague or promotional language. We aim to explain them in a more useful way. Our slot coverage considers how a game is structured, how its features work and what the stated statistics may mean for a player.


RTP

RTP, or return to player, is a long-term theoretical percentage calculated over a large number of spins. It does not predict short-term results and it does not guarantee that an individual player will recover a set amount. We explain RTP carefully because misunderstanding it can lead to unrealistic expectations.


Volatility

Volatility describes the general risk profile of a slot. Lower volatility games may produce smaller wins more frequently, while higher volatility games may produce longer dry spells with the possibility of larger feature wins. We consider volatility when describing how a slot may feel to play, while making clear that outcomes remain random.
