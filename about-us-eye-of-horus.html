Our Experience with Casino Games, Slots and Mechanics

Casino content is only useful when it explains the games properly. Eye Of Horus covers slots and casino games by looking at how the underlying mechanics affect gameplay, risk and expectations.


In slot reviews and game guides, we pay attention to practical details such as:


  • RTP: the theoretical long-term return to player, including whether a game has multiple RTP versions;
  • volatility: how frequently a slot may pay and how prize sizes may vary;
  • hit frequency: where available, how often any win may occur over the long term;
  • bonus features: free spins, expanding symbols, wilds, scatters, multipliers, gamble features and bonus buys;
  • paylines and ways to win: whether the game uses fixed lines, adjustable lines, cluster pays or alternative win systems;
  • stake range: minimum and maximum bet settings and how they may affect risk;
  • feature behaviour: whether the bonus round is random, symbol-triggered, meter-based or purchased where allowed.

We also explain that RTP is a statistical model measured across very large numbers of spins. It does not predict what will happen in any individual session, and it does not remove the risk of losing money.
