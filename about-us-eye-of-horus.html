Casino Games, Slots, RTP and Volatility

Much of our editorial work focuses on how casino games actually function. For slots, that means looking beyond themes and headline features to explain the mechanics that affect risk and gameplay.


Our content may consider:


  • RTP: the theoretical return to player over a very large number of spins, not a prediction of what will happen in one session;
  • volatility: how frequently wins may land and how varied the prize sizes can be;
  • paylines and ways to win: how symbols are evaluated and how winning combinations are formed;
  • bonus rounds: free spins, pick features, expanding symbols, gamble features, respins and hold-and-win mechanics;
  • maximum exposure: the importance of stake size, bankroll limits and session control;
  • game versions: whether RTP variants or market-specific settings may differ between casinos.

We explain these points in plain English because they matter. A high-volatility slot can feel very different from a low-volatility game, and a bonus feature can be entertaining without improving the likelihood of a positive outcome.
